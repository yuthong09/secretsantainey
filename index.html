🎅 Secret Santa: Good Gift / Bad Gift

Draw two names. Give one great gift and one gloriously terrible one.

How it works

Everyone draws two different people:

🎁 GOOD GIFT

Buy something they'll actually love. You'll see their wish list to help you choose.

Budget: RM [TBC]

💩 BAD GIFT

Buy the funniest, most useless, most "why would you do this" gift you can find. Name only – no wish list, just creativity.

Budget: RM [TBC]

🏆 The best of the worst gift wins a RM100 voucher! Everyone votes on the day.

The rules

  1. Your good-gift person and bad-gift person are always two different people, and you'll never draw yourself.
  2. Everyone gives exactly one good gift and one bad gift, and receives exactly one of each.
  3. Keep it secret. Don't tell anyone who you drew – not even the organisers (they don't know either).
  4. Stick to the budget for each gift.
  5. Wrap your gifts and label them clearly: "GOOD – To: [name]" and "BAD – To: [name]". Don't write your own name.
  6. Bad gifts should be funny, not offensive. Nothing unsafe, nothing perishable that'll smell by the exchange, nothing that would embarrass someone in front of the team.
  7. The bad gift with the most votes wins the voucher (prize goes to the giver – reveal yourself only if you win 😉).
